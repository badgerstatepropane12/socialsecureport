Make the Most of Your Personal my Social Security Account!

For earlier and more convenient access to your 2027 Cost-of-Living-Adjustment (COLA) notice, choose paperless notifications in your personal my Social Security account by November 18.

Going paperless makes it easier to stay informed as soon as information is available. You'll receive an email when important notices are available, allowing you to view them securely through your account without waiting for mail delivery.

How to Switch to Paperless: 

  1. Visit ssa.gov/myaccount.  
  2. Select paperless notifications to receive your notices online.
